      How BoreWise reads a borewell

      1. 1 · ChirpSend a low sweep

        A 60–250 Hz chirp travels down a 25 mm sounding tube. Low tones survive the 150 m round trip; kHz tones die in the tube.

      2. 2 · EchoFind the water

        Cross-correlation picks the echo off the water surface. Depth is c·τ/2, corrected for air temperature and tube width.

      3. 3 · RefillTest the aquifer

        When the pump stops, the refill curve is a free pump test. Its slope gives transmissivity; the first drop gives well efficiency.

      4. 4 · ForecastName the day

        Seasonal trend, peak drawdown and intake depth give a dry-up window and the rationing that carries you to the monsoon.
