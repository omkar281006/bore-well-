## Acoustic Ranging R&D Assessment

Attempts to measure deep water tables using low-cost acoustic/ultrasonic transducers encounter severe physical acoustic constraints:
1. **Signal Attenuation:** Sound pulses decay rapidly over deep distances (50m–150m) inside narrow borewell casings.
2. **Internal Wall Echoes & Reflections:** Pipe joints, power cables, and pump riser pipes produce multi-path echo interference.
3. **Pumping & Mechanical Noise:** Active submerged pumps create intense acoustic background noise that corrupts pulse-time-of-flight measurements.
4. **Humidity & Temperature Gradients:** Sound speed varies non-linearly with humidity and temperature down the well shaft ($c \approx 331.3 \sqrt{1 + T/273.15}$ m/s).

## Analytical Assumptions

- **Simplified Mass-Balance Aquifer Model:** Drawdown and exponential recovery equations ($dS/dt = -k \cdot (S - S_0)$) represent single-well diagnostic proxies, not full 3D hydrodynamic numerical models (such as MODFLOW).
- **Quantile Stochastic Forecasts:** P10, P50, and P90 forecast horizons estimate drawdown trends based on historical pump duty cycles and Monte Carlo trajectory simulations. They are decision-support indicators, not guaranteed deterministic failure predictors.
